Luxury perfume advertisement, premium beauty campaign, photorealistic, editorial fashion photography, 9:16 vertical composition.

Main Character:
20-year-old East Asian woman.

Bottle World Concept:
A gigantic luxury crystal perfume bottle contains an enchanting miniature world. The heroine naturally lives inside the bottle rather than being trapped in it.

Bottle Design:
Tall sapphire-blue crystal perfume bottle with an elegant oval silhouette, museum-grade crystal glass, thick transparent crystal with luxurious optical refraction, polished white-gold metal details, brilliant-cut crystal cap, premium French niche perfume aesthetic.

Bottle Space DNA (Hard Lock):
Character occupies about 70% of the bottle interior, leaving generous breathing space for flowers, pearls, floating light particles and crystal reflections.

Pose DNA (Hard Lock):
Side Sitting Pose.
The heroine sits elegantly with both legs folded naturally to one side on a fully bloomed blue hydrangea, never touching the bottle base.
One hand gently supports her beside the flower while the other rests softly on her lap.
Her posture remains relaxed with graceful S-shaped body lines.

Floral Theme:
Blue hydrangea, white delphinium, forget-me-not flowers, crystal blossoms, floating petals naturally blooming throughout the bottle.

Luxury Color DNA:
Primary palette:
Sapphire Blue × Ice White × White Gold.
Accent colors:
Soft aqua glow, iridescent blue reflections, luminous crystal highlights.
High brightness, medium-low saturation with selective vibrant sapphire accents to maintain a premium luxury perfume aesthetic.

Materials:
Crystal glass, pearls, silk organza, transparent petals, luxury jewelry, white-gold craftsmanship.

Fragrance Visualization:
Floating pearls, crystal light refractions, glowing particles, translucent petals, elegant flowing light ribbons instead of smoke.

Lighting:
Bright frontal lighting.
Face and eyes are the brightest focal point.
Clean crystal reflections.

No harsh shadows.
