Luxury perfume advertisement, premium beauty campaign, photorealistic, editorial fashion photography, 9:16 vertical composition.

Main Character:
20-year-old East Asian woman.

Bottle World Concept:
A gigantic luxury crystal perfume bottle contains an enchanting miniature world. The heroine naturally lives inside the bottle rather than being trapped in it.

Bottle Design:
Elegant amethyst crystal perfume bottle with a rounded couture silhouette, museum-grade crystal glass, thick transparent crystal with luxurious violet optical refraction, polished rose-gold metal details, faceted jewel cap, luxurious haute couture fragrance aesthetic.

Bottle Space DNA (Hard Lock):
Character occupies about 70% of the bottle interior, leaving generous breathing space for flowers, pearls, floating light particles and crystal reflections.

Pose DNA (Hard Lock):
Mermaid Pose.
The heroine sits gracefully on a fully bloomed lavender blossom with both legs elegantly folded to one side in a flowing mermaid-like silhouette, elevated above the bottle base.
One hand lightly touches the flower while the other gently rests beside her.
Relaxed shoulders and refined posture create elegant couture fashion lines.

Floral Theme:
Lavender, violet flowers, lilac blossoms, purple iris, delicate translucent petals naturally growing inside the bottle.

Luxury Color DNA:
Primary palette:
Amethyst Purple × Soft Lavender × Rose Gold.
Accent colors:
Pastel violet glow, iridescent crystal reflections, warm golden highlights.
High brightness, medium-low saturation with selective vibrant purple accents to maintain a premium luxury perfume aesthetic.

Materials:
Crystal glass, pearls, silk organza, transparent petals, luxury jewelry, rose-gold craftsmanship.

Fragrance Visualization:
Floating pearls, crystal light refractions, glowing particles, translucent petals, elegant flowing light ribbons instead of smoke.

Lighting:
Bright frontal lighting.
Face and eyes are the brightest focal point.
Clean crystal reflections.
Soft luminous background, slightly darker than the subject.
No harsh shadows.

Composition:
Luxury perfume campaign.
Character occupies about 70% of the frame.
Bottle fills about 80–85% of the frame with partial crop for a close-up editorial look.
