Luxury perfume advertisement, premium beauty campaign, photorealistic, editorial fashion photography, 9:16 vertical composition.

Main Character:
20-year-old East Asian woman.

Bottle World Concept:
A gigantic luxury crystal perfume bottle contains an enchanting miniature world. The heroine naturally lives inside the bottle rather than being trapped in it.

Bottle Design:
Elegant aqua crystal perfume bottle inspired by flowing water, museum-grade crystal glass, thick transparent crystal with luxurious optical refraction, polished silver metal details, transparent faceted crystal cap, modern luxury perfume aesthetic.

Bottle Space DNA (Hard Lock):
Character occupies about 70% of the bottle interior, leaving generous breathing space for flowers, pearls, floating light particles and crystal reflections.

Pose DNA (Hard Lock):
Floating Pose.
The heroine floats weightlessly just above a fully bloomed white lotus without directly touching the flower or the bottle base.
Her legs are softly bent with pointed toes, creating a graceful floating silhouette.
One hand reaches gently toward the glowing petals while the other rests near her waist.
Flowing fabrics, relaxed shoulders and elongated body lines create an ethereal sense of lightness and serenity.

Floral Theme:
White lotus, water lily, baby's breath, translucent aquatic blossoms naturally floating inside the bottle.

Luxury Color DNA:
Primary palette:
Aqua Blue × Crystal White × Silver.
Accent colors:
Turquoise glow, iridescent water reflections, luminous crystal highlights.
High brightness, medium-low saturation with selective fresh aqua accents to maintain a premium luxury perfume aesthetic.

Materials:
Crystal glass, pearls, silk organza, transparent petals, luxury jewelry, polished silver craftsmanship.

Fragrance Visualization:
Floating pearls, crystal light refractions, glowing particles, translucent petals, elegant flowing light ribbons instead of smoke.

Lighting:
Bright frontal lighting.
Face and eyes are the brightest focal point.
Clean crystal reflections.
Soft luminous background, slightly darker than the subject.
No harsh shadows.

Composition:
Luxury perfume campaign.
Character occupies about 70% of the frame.
Bottle fills about 80–85% of the frame with partial crop for a close-up editorial look.
