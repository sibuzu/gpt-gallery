Luxury perfume advertisement, premium beauty campaign, photorealistic, editorial fashion photography, 9:16 vertical composition.

Main Character:
20-year-old East Asian woman.

Bottle World Concept:
A gigantic luxury crystal perfume bottle contains an enchanting miniature world. The heroine naturally lives inside the bottle rather than being trapped in it.

Bottle Design:
Large round ruby crystal perfume bottle with a luxurious vintage silhouette, museum-grade crystal glass, thick transparent crystal with rich ruby optical refraction, champagne-gold metal details, jewel-quality crystal cap, premium haute parfumerie aesthetic.

Bottle Space DNA (Hard Lock):
Character occupies about 70% of the bottle interior, leaving generous breathing space for flowers, pearls, floating light particles and crystal reflections.

Pose DNA (Hard Lock):
One Knee Raised Pose.
The heroine sits gracefully on a fully bloomed red camellia, elevated above the bottle base.
One knee is gently raised toward her chest while the other leg extends naturally with a soft bend.
One arm loosely embraces the raised knee while the other hand rests delicately on the flower.
Relaxed shoulders, elongated neck, elegant S-shaped body lines, effortless haute couture elegance.

Floral Theme:
Red camellia, deep crimson rose, peony buds, delicate cherry blossoms naturally flourishing inside the bottle.

Luxury Color DNA:
Primary palette:
Ruby Red × Ivory White × Champagne Gold.
Accent colors:
Soft blush glow, ruby crystal reflections, luminous golden highlights.
High brightness, medium-low saturation with selective rich ruby accents to maintain a premium luxury perfume aesthetic.

Materials:
Crystal glass, pearls, silk organza, transparent petals, luxury jewelry, champagne gold craftsmanship.

Fragrance Visualization:
Floating pearls, crystal light refractions, glowing particles, translucent petals, elegant flowing light ribbons instead of smoke.

Lighting:
Bright frontal lighting.
Face and eyes are the brightest focal point.
Clean crystal reflections.
Soft luminous background, slightly darker than the subject.
No harsh shadows.

Composition:
Luxury perfume campaign.
Character occupies about 70% of the frame.
Bottle fills about 80–85% of the frame with partial crop for a close-up editorial look.
