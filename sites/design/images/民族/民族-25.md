[Thai-inspired bandeau mini dress with wrapped silk skirt]

She is wearing an extremely airy summer mini dress inspired by Thai traditional silk sabai and pha sin elements, redesigned as a sensual contemporary resort couture look. The top uses a single translucent silk sabai sash crossing diagonally over a structured embroidered bandeau, fully covering the bust while leaving one shoulder, collarbones, upper back, and waist beautifully open. The high-waisted mini skirt is formed from feather-light silk panels wrapped around the hips with a short side opening for cool movement, tastefully covering the important areas with layered fabric placement. Delicate gold thread borders, tiny temple-flower embroidery, and subtle jewel accents reference Thai court elegance without heaviness. Colors transition from jasmine white to lotus pink, Thai gold, pale turquoise, soft lilac, and warm pearl.
