20-year-old East Asian woman.

Bottle World Concept:
A gigantic luxury crystal perfume bottle contains an enchanting miniature world. The heroine naturally lives inside the bottle rather than being trapped in it.

Large emerald-cut crystal perfume bottle, museum-grade crystal glass, thick transparent crystal with luxurious optical refraction, emerald-green crystal gradients, brushed platinum metal details, faceted gemstone cap, ultra-luxury haute parfumerie aesthetic.

Bottle Space DNA (Hard Lock):
Character occupies about 70% of the bottle interior, leaving generous breathing space for flowers, pearls, floating light particles and crystal reflections.

Pose DNA (Hard Lock):
Cross-Legged Pose.
The heroine sits gracefully with her legs crossed on a fully bloomed white orchid, elevated above the bottle base.
Her spine remains naturally elongated with relaxed shoulders.

White orchid, lily of the valley, jasmine blossoms, crystal ivy, delicate botanical elements naturally flourishing inside the bottle.
